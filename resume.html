Who I am

Summary

Silvia YU is a content strategist, new media operator, and visual communicator with an interdisciplinary background in applied psychology and digital education. Her work sits at the intersection of psychology, digital experiences, and technology — spanning content planning, bilingual editing, social media operations, visual design, UI/UX, user research, and project coordination across higher education, mental health, wellbeing, and digital-product contexts. She enjoys moving between research and making: understanding people, structuring information, designing experiences, and turning ideas into something tangible — whether that means building an interactive learning activity, shaping a wellbeing product, or creating communication materials that connect human needs with thoughtful digital solutions.
